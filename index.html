    Rules reference

    Match the current mission's pattern with your modules and complete 7 missions to win. Patterns can't be rotated or flipped. Play a card from your hand for its movement points (or the bigger number under its pattern if the modules already match it). Unspent points are lost when you play another card or complete a mission.

    Movement (1 point each)

    Drag a module onto a lit-up space, or click it and then click the space.

    ModuleArrowMoves

    Abilities (free, once each)

    When you're not moving a module or resolving another ability, flip an active (★) module to its spent (✕) side and resolve its ability. Click the ★ strip on a card, or select the module and press A.

    After every move and ability

    • Modules never stack on each other.
    • They stay on the grid of the starting 2×3 layout.
    • They form one connected group. Touching edge-to-edge or corner-to-corner counts as adjacent.

    The board only offers moves that follow these rules.

    After a mission

    Cover the old mission with the top deck card, then draw 1 card if you hold fewer than 5. If the deck runs out, flip the discard pile over without shuffling. You lose if you can't complete your current mission.
